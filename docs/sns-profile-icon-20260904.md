# SNSプロフィールアイコン

方式: 組み込み ImageGen（新規生成後、背景と余白を編集）。アプリ本体のアイコンは変更していません。

成果物: `public/pr/assets/sns-profile-icon-20260904.png`

## 初回プロンプト

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Finished square social media profile icon for a Japanese personal budgeting app.
Primary request: One beautiful minimal white household ledger notebook symbol with a clear blue yen symbol "¥" on its cover, centered on a full-bleed solid Material blue #1565C0 square background.
Style/medium: Professional crisp flat vector-like raster brand mark, welcoming rounded geometry, restrained Material Design aesthetic, optically balanced, bold silhouette readable at 32 pixels.
Composition: Single front-facing upright white ledger notebook with softly rounded corners and a subtle blue spine detail. A large blue "¥" is centered on the white cover. The complete mark stays inside the central 60 percent of the square so circular avatar cropping never clips it. Clean generous blue negative space.
Palette: Only white and #1565C0 blue.
Text: Only the symbol "¥", exactly once. No app name or other text.
Constraints: Deliver one finished icon, square 1:1, preferably 1024x1024. Background fills every edge, no external frame, no rounded-corner canvas, no mockup or presentation sheet. No gradients, shadows, 3D effects, tiny decorations, charts, coins, hands, watermarks, or extra symbols.
```

## 最終編集プロンプト

```text
Edit the provided icon into a finished social profile avatar. Preserve its white notebook and blue ¥ identity. IMPORTANT: replace the entire black or transparent background with opaque solid vivid Material blue #1565C0, filling the whole square canvas edge to edge. Reduce the notebook mark so its height is 58% of the canvas height, centered both horizontally and vertically, with generous blue margins on every side. This should be a blue square with a smaller white notebook at the center, safe to crop to a circle. Simplify to flat solid white and blue; no texture, gradients, shadows, mockup, or additional text. Keep the blue ¥ visible on the white cover. Output one square image.
```
